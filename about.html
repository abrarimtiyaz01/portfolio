I’m a final-year B.Tech student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at the Islamic University of Science and Technology (IUST), Pulwama.

How I work

My ECE background gives me a foundation in circuits and embedded systems. Alongside it, I’ve developed projects in machine learning and web development, learning by building and iterating.

I’m especially interested in the space where hardware and software meet: sensing a real-world signal, making sense of the data, and presenting something useful to people.

Through training with IIT Bombay’s e-Yantra program and an ML/AI internship at NIT Srinagar, I’ve explored Linux workflows, programming, and applied machine learning.

What I bring

Curiosity, a hands-on engineering mindset, and the ability to work across a project’s different layers—from an Arduino prototype to a model-backed interface.
